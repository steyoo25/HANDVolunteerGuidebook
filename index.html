About Duke HAND

Duke HAND (Duke Helping Hands for Alzheimer's and other Neurological Disorders) seeks to provide our members with real-world experience in end-of-life care through volunteering with dementia care partners in the Durham community. We also aim to start important conversations among our members about attitudes toward death and dying, and the treatment of seniors and those with neurological disorders.

As a volunteer, your role is vital—you provide companionship, engagement, and dignity to residents who need it most. This guidebook will equip you with knowledge and practical skills to make a real difference.
