About Duke HAND

Duke HAND (Duke Helping Hands for Alzheimer's and other Neurological Disorders) provides real-world volunteering experience in dementia care with community partners in the Durham area. We create meaningful opportunities for volunteers to engage with seniors, build compassion, and foster important conversations about aging, death, dying, and the dignity we owe to those living with neurological conditions.

As a Duke HAND volunteer, you play a vital role—providing companionship, engagement, and human connection to residents during a vulnerable time of life. This guidebook equips you with knowledge, techniques, and practical strategies grounded in research and real-world experience. Whether through conversation, music, creative storytelling, or craft activities, your presence and attention create meaningful moments that improve residents' quality of life.
